About Layton's Baits

Real fishing. Real baits. Real purpose.

Layton's Baits wasn't built to be some big corporate fishing brand. It started with a regular fisherman who wanted to make baits that actually worked in real water and real conditions.

Every bait is hand-poured with attention to color, action, scent, and detail — not just to look good in a pack, but to get bit when it matters.

This brand is about more than selling baits. It's about time on the water, family, memories, and giving other anglers something made by someone who actually fishes.

What we stand for
